## What's Happening?

- The U.S. Strategic Petroleum Reserve (SPR) — the emergency oil stockpile — is at its **lowest level in decades**. <span class="c-pending">source pending</span>
- Administration policy let oil companies export more oil and delayed refilling the reserve, leaving the country without its primary buffer against a crisis. <span class="c-pending">source pending</span>

### Why Is This Trump's Fault?

- His administration sold oil out of the reserve and gave exporters more freedom instead of keeping oil for emergencies.
- If a war or supply disaster hits, America faces **sky-high gas prices** and shortages with no cushion.

### What Happens Next?

If gas prices spike, right-wing media will blame Democrats — even though these policies set the stage.
