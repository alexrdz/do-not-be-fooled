## What's Happening?

- The "One Big Beautiful Bill Act" (OBBBA) is deeply unpopular — largely because it **cuts Medicaid**, the healthcare program for low-income Americans.
- Polls show only **38%** of Americans support the bill. Even most Republicans dislike the Medicaid cuts.

| Fact | Source |

### Why Is This Trump's Fault?

Trump and his allies pushed the bill and called it a "win for America" — but it mostly helps the wealthy while hurting regular people.

### What Happens Next?

When people lose healthcare or struggle financially, right-wing media will blame Democrats — even though the bill was Trump's signature policy.
