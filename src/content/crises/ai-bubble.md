## What's Happening?

- Big Tech companies (Microsoft, Google, Meta, Amazon) are spending **over $700 billion** on AI data centers in 2026 alone — more than the GDP of most countries.
- NVIDIA, the company making AI chips, is now the most valuable company on the stock market, worth over **$4.5 trillion**.
- Experts warn this is a **bubble bigger than the dot-com crash** of the early 2000s. If it pops, millions could lose jobs and savings.

### Why Is This Trump's Fault?

- Trump pushed for "no rules" on AI and bragged about making America "#1 in AI," encouraging reckless spending.
- His administration blocked the regulations that could have slowed the bubble down.
